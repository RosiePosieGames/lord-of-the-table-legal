Rosie Posie Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Rosie Posie Games LLC operates Lord of the Table ("the Game"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices available to players.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect or process:

  • Account information: Your chosen username or player ID and a hashed password used to authenticate your account.
  • Gameplay information: Game results, scores, multiplayer matches, saved progress, card designs or unlocks, challenges, and other gameplay activity.
  • Virtual currency information: Your Coffers balance, in-game rewards, duel entry wagers, and records of relevant transactions.
  • Purchase information: Records needed to verify and manage in-app purchases, if purchases are available.
  • Technical information: Connection information, server logs, and diagnostic or security information that may be processed by our hosting and database providers.
  • Advertising information: If advertising is introduced, advertising providers may process ad interactions, device identifiers, or other technical information according to the services used.

Passwords are intended to be stored as secure hashes rather than plain-text passwords.

Lord of the Table does not currently provide an in-game chat system. The Game does not request access to your contacts, photos, or personal files as a core game feature.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and authenticate player accounts.
  • Save player profiles and game progress.
  • Operate multiplayer matchmaking, duels, scores, and leaderboards.
  • Manage Coffers, rewards, card designs, and relevant purchase records.
  • Protect accounts, prevent abuse, and maintain server security.
  • Diagnose technical problems and maintain game performance.
  • Support advertising or analytics features if they are introduced.

3. Where Information Is Stored

The Game uses server hosting and database services to operate. These services currently include Render and Neon, subject to the production configuration in use.

Information may be processed or stored by these providers as necessary to operate the Game, maintain accounts, save progress, and protect the service.

4. Coffers and In-Game Purchases

Coffers are virtual in-game currency. They may be used for eligible in-game features, including entering duels and obtaining card designs.

Coffers have no cash value. They cannot be cashed out, redeemed for real money, or exchanged for money outside the Game. They are intended for use only within Lord of the Table.

If in-app purchases are offered, purchase information may be processed to verify transactions and provide the purchased items or currency. Payment details are handled according to the applicable payment provider's practices.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share or permit processing of information by service providers when reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the Game.

These providers may include:

  • Hosting and database providers.
  • Payment or app-store providers, if purchases are offered.
  • Advertising providers, if ads are introduced.
  • Other technical service providers used to operate the Game.

Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable contractual or legal requirements. We will update this policy if our data practices change.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Game, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Players can initiate account deletion through the in-game account deletion feature. We will delete or de-identify account information as appropriate, subject to information we are legally required to retain.

Deleting an account may affect access to saved progress, virtual currency, and other account-related features. Any applicable purchase-related obligations will be handled according to the relevant platform's rules.

7. Children's Privacy

Lord of the Table is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect account information and game data. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published with a revised last-updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or account-related requests, contact: RosiePosieGamesLLC@gmail.com
